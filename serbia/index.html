EV charging in Serbia

Serbia is where BlokVolt has the most detail: a map of every public charger with prices, a register of 51 installers and sellers with their published prices, public charging networks, EPS electricity tariffs, subsidies and step-by-step guides for charging in an apartment building. All of it is on blokvolt.rs — in English, Serbian and Russian.

In short

Subsidy 2026€5,000 for a new battery-electric car (categories M1 and N1), €500 for a motorcycle, €250 for a moped; applications through eUprava until 1 December 2026. Budget 170 million RSD; about 600 vehicles funded by August.
Electricity at homeRegulated EPS household prices, with network fees, excise and VAT, range from 4.15 to 38.26 RSD per kWh depending on the consumption zone and tariff. The cheaper night tariff runs at different hours by region.
Public charging pricesOperators do not publish price lists; most commercial networks charge per minute. DC receipts collected by the editors work out at 43–79 RSD per kWh.
Apartment buildingsA charger on the common parts of a building needs a decision of the residents' assembly (skupština stambene zajednice). The guide on blokvolt.rs explains the steps and has a template decision.
