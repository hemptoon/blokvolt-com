EV charging in Bosnia and Herzegovina

Bosnia and Herzegovina is one of Europe's smallest EV markets: 763 electric vehicles were registered at the end of 2024, and Eurostat-based figures show 71 new battery-electric cars in 2025 (0.7% of new car registrations). Public charging is split between the utility Elektroprivreda BiH, private networks such as e-GO and single chargers at hotels, malls and fuel stations; most operators publish no prices, there is no official nationwide count and no Tesla Supercharger. Only a few firms publish home-charger prices (about 670–2,900 KM per wallbox), household electricity is regulated separately in the Federation, Republika Srpska and Brčko District, and the Federation's 2026 EV purchase subsidy (up to 12,000 KM) was fully reserved within two days.

What the law says about a charger in a shared garage or parking lot — who has to agree, who pays for the electricity and how it is metered.

  • Work on common parts for an owner's own installation (Federation of BiH)An owner may change their own unit, but if the change requires work on common parts of the building it is allowed only if it is usual or serves an important interest of the owner; otherwise any co-owner can forbid it. Co-owners cannot, however, forbid laying lines for light, gas, energy, water and telephone. The law does not mention EV chargers.Law: Zakon o stvarnim pravima FBiH (Službene novine FBiH 66/13, 100/13, 32/19), Art. 95Source: Zakon o stvarnim pravima (FBiH) – Paragraf.ba · no date on page; checked 23.09.2026 · high reliability
