EV charging in Slovenia

Slovenia's electric car market grew quickly in 2025–2026: battery-electric cars were about 11% of new passenger cars in 2025 and almost 20% in January–August 2026, while Borzen offered purchase subsidies of up to €7,800 that were oversubscribed by August 2026. Home wallboxes are sold by energy companies, car importers, electrical installers and online shops; several sellers quote prices with basic installation at the reduced 9.5% VAT rate, and a separate Borzen grant covers 55% of the cost up to €1,000 per charging point, with requests already above the 2026 budget. Public charging is led by Petrol and Elektro Ljubljana's Gremo na elektriko network, alongside IONITY, Tesla and MOONcharge; published prices run from €0.22–0.35/kWh for AC and €0.45/kWh for DC at Gremo na elektriko to €0.43–0.73/kWh at IONITY.
