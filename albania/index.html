EV charging in Albania

Albania's electric-car fleet almost doubled in 2025: Eurostat counts 10,938 battery-electric passenger cars at the end of the year, 91% more than a year earlier, and 5,517 new registrations. New battery-electric cars are VAT-exempt according to the latest source, although sources conflict, and electric vehicles pay no annual vehicle tax. Public charging is young and fragmented: VEGA lists 20+ sites, PlugoAL five 280 kW sites, and the state supplier OSHEE announced a national network for 2026, with no station found operating on 28.09.2026. No network publishes a per-kWh price on its website; OSHEE estimated in November 2025 that charging stations sell electricity at 40–60 lek per kWh. At home, a kilowatt-hour costs 10.2 lek with VAT while the household stays within 700 kWh a month.

Subsidies

Open VAT exemption for new battery-electric vehicles

Amount: 20% VAT not charged at import or first sale

Who: Buyers of new, zero-km battery-electric vehicles never registered in any country (not used EVs or hybrids)

Latest source: ALTAX (23.10.2024), citing Law 92/2014 on VAT, art. 51(p); the law text itself could not be read. Albanian Customs says VAT on imports of new electric cars was removed in 2020 (543 cars imported VAT-free in 2020–2022). Sources conflict: a government statement reported by Albeu (20.04.2022) spoke of a cut from 20% to 6%, and the Ministry of Finance's 2024 fiscal bulletin lists only a 6% rate for electric buses. Customs duty on cars consists only of VAT; no EV-specific excise rule was found.

Source: ALTAX – VAT treatment of electric vehicles · 23.10.2024 · medium reliability
Open Exemption from the annual vehicle tax (TVMP)

Amount: full exemption

Who: Electric vehicles

DPSHTRR lists electric vehicles among the exempt vehicles without citing the legal act; vehicles aged 0–3 years are also exempt.

Source: DPSHTRR – vehicles exempt from the annual tax · no date on page; checked 28.09.2026 · high reliability
Unconfirmed Refund of first-registration fees

Amount: 7,500 lek in total: physical inspection 1,500, circulation permit 1,000, standard plates 3,000, ownership certificate 2,000

Who: Electric and plug-in vehicles at first registration, since November 2019

Reported on 15.10.2019; Business Magazine Albania (21.04.2022) says the refund applies from November 2019. Basis cited: Council of Ministers decision no. 412 of 19.06.2019. Not confirmed for 2026: the DPSHTRR registration page does not mention electric vehicles, and the latest source repeating the refund is a government statement of 20.04.2022.

Source: Shqiptarja.com – Albania removes fees for electric vehicles · 15.10.2019 · low reliability
No scheme Purchase grant

No purchase subsidy found for 2026, and no municipal benefit (free parking, bus lanes, taxi relief) found in Tirana (checked 28.09.2026). A DPSHTRR page on electric taxis mentions reduced taxes without citing a legal act.

Charging in an apartment building

What the law says about a charger in a shared garage or parking lot — who has to agree, who pays for the electricity and how it is metered.

Apartment buildings

Albania's co-ownership law does not mention EV chargers, garages as such, or an owner's right to install a charger at their own cost. Parking areas and basements are common property unless the ownership act says otherwise (art. 6(2)). Ordinary improvements need a majority of participation quotas (art. 39(1)); major installations such as heating systems, lifts, generators and 'other improvements of this nature' need at least 75% (art. 39(2)); changing the look of common parts needs prior written approval by a simple majority of votes and must follow planning law (art. 40(2)–(3)). Which rule applies to a charger in a shared garage is not stated.

Law: Ligji nr. 55/2025 'Për administrimin e bashkëpronësisë në ndërtesa', adopted 03.07.2025 (Decree no. 282 of 30.07.2025); it repealed Law 10 112/2009 and enters into force 15 days after publication in the Official Journal

Source: Law no. 55/2025 on the administration of co-ownership in buildings · 30.07.2025 · high reliability

National plans and EU rules

  • The draft National Energy and Climate Plan, put to public consultation, aims for 10% of vehicles in circulation to be electric by 2030 and plans chargers on national roads, at border crossings and in tourist areas; its adoption was not verified. Source: shqiptarja.com · 04.01.2025 · medium reliability
  • The Albanian Energy Efficiency Agency tendered the installation and operation of public chargers in eight cities and near border crossings (83.3 million lek) in June 2023; the outcome was not verified. Source: albanianpost.com · 07.06.2023 · medium reliability
  • No Albanian law or draft law on charging or alternative-fuel infrastructure, and no act aligning with the EU AFIR regulation, was found. checked 28.09.2026 · medium reliability

What could not be verified

Known gaps. If you can fill one with a public source, write to hello@blokvolt.com.

  • No network publishes a per-kWh price on its website: PlugoAL and iCharge show prices only in their apps, VEGA and DAB Energy publish none, and OSHEE Charge has announced no price.
  • Site counts per network could not be verified in Open Charge Map (API blocked).
  • No source says how many of Albania's electric cars were imported used; INSTAT and DPSHTRR give no end-2025 total, and OSHEE's figure of about 16,000 electric vehicles (November 2025) is not reconciled with Eurostat's 10,938.
  • VAT on new electric cars: the text of Law 92/2014, art. 51 could not be read, and sources conflict (exemption per Customs and ALTAX; a cut from 20% to 6% per a 2022 government statement).
  • Household VAT: the 20% added to the ERE prices comes from media reports; the ERE table does not state it.
  • A1 (Rruga e Kombit) toll: the official car tariff is €5 with VAT (ATRAKO, undated), payable in lek at the Bank of Albania rate of the day; the 660 lek reported for payment in lek (Timoni.al 2024, tolls.eu 2026) is not confirmed by an official source.
  • VEGA's DC chargers have both GB/T and CCS2 sockets, but no source says how many cars in Albania use GB/T.
  • No fast charger on the A1 (Rruga e Kombit) itself was confirmed.
  • OSHEE Charge: no operating station was found on 28.09.2026; a first-phase figure of 100–120 stations appears only in an opinion article (Shqiptarja, 16.11.2025).
