EV charging in Albania

Albania has the fastest-growing electric-car fleet in the region: Eurostat counts 10,938 battery-electric passenger cars at the end of 2025, 91% more than a year earlier, most of them imported used cars. New electric vehicles are VAT-exempt and pay no annual vehicle tax. Public charging is young and fragmented: VEGA runs 20+ sites, PlugoAL five 280 kW sites, and the state supplier OSHEE plans a national network in 2026. No network publishes a per-kWh price on its website; OSHEE says drivers typically pay 40–60 lek per kWh. At home, a kilowatt-hour costs 10.2 lek with VAT.

Subsidies

Open VAT exemption for new battery-electric vehicles

Amount: 20% VAT not charged at import or first sale

Who: Buyers of new, never-registered battery-electric vehicles (not used EVs or hybrids)

Customs duty on cars consists only of VAT, so a new EV is imported tax-free.

Source: ALTAX – VAT treatment of electric vehicles · 23.10.2024 · medium reliability
Open Exemption from the annual vehicle tax (TVMP)

Amount: full exemption

Who: Electric vehicles

Source: DPSHTRR – vehicles exempt from the annual tax · high reliability
Unconfirmed Refund of first-registration fees

Amount: fees for inspection, circulation permit, plates and ownership certificate

Who: Electric vehicles, since November 2019

We could not confirm that the refund still applies in 2026.

Source: Business Magazine Albania · 21.04.2022 · low reliability

National plans and EU rules

  • The draft National Energy and Climate Plan aims for 10% of vehicles in circulation to be electric by 2030. Source: shqiptarja.com · 04.01.2025 · medium reliability
  • The Albanian Energy Efficiency Agency tendered the installation and operation of public chargers (83.3 million lek) in 2023. Source: albanianpost.com · 07.06.2023 · medium reliability
