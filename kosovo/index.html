EV charging in Kosovo

Kosovo had 1,457 battery-electric passenger cars at the end of 2025 (Eurostat), 51% more than a year earlier, and 539 new ones were registered that year. Imports are rising: 780 electric cars came in during January–August 2026, 665 of them from China. Electric cars get no tax break — they pay the same import taxes as petrol and diesel cars: 10% customs duty, 18% VAT and, on used cars, excise by age. ECHARGE is the only network that publishes prices: €0.39–0.49 per kWh on DC and €0.21 (day) / €0.19 (night) on AC, without saying whether VAT is included. At home, night-rate electricity costs about 4.2 euro cents per kWh with VAT while the household stays within 800 kWh a month.

Subsidies

No scheme Tax relief for electric vehicles

Electric cars pay the same import taxes as petrol and diesel cars: 10% customs duty, 18% VAT and, on used cars, a fixed excise of about €400–700 by age (Gazeta Express, 27.09.2026; Radio Free Europe, 06.04.2026). RTV21 (06.09.2025) reported €400 for an electric car and €10 for a hybrid; the customs tariff itself could not be read, so the exact excise is not confirmed. On 28.08.2024 the Government approved a zero registration tax for electric cars in the draft Law on Vehicles, but in April 2026 Radio Free Europe reported no import or registration relief. The Kosovo Chamber of Commerce has asked to remove the customs duty and cut VAT to 8%; the ministries did not answer the newspaper's questions.

Source: Gazeta Express – China takes the wheel in Kosovo's EV market · 27.09.2026 · medium reliability
No scheme Purchase grant

No purchase subsidy and no municipal benefit found (checked 28.09.2026).

Charging in an apartment building

What the law says about a charger in a shared garage or parking lot — who has to agree, who pays for the electricity and how it is metered.

Apartment buildings

The condominium law in force does not mention garages, parking or EV chargers. Ordinary decisions of the owners' association need owners holding more than 50% of the co-ownership (art. 14); works beyond regular administration, including changes to the co-owned parts, need the consent of all owners (art. 32). Which of the two applies to a charger in a shared garage is not stated.

Law: Ligji nr. 03/L-091 për shfrytëzimin, administrimin dhe mirëmbajtjen e ndërtesës në bashkëpronësi, arts. 14, 28 and 32 (adopted 12.03.2009; Official Gazette no. 51, 15.04.2009; no amendment shown)

Source: Official Gazette – Law No. 03/L-091 · checked 28.09.2026 · high reliability

National plans and EU rules

  • The Energy Strategy 2022–2031, as reviewed, sets no numeric target for electric vehicles or charging stations; Radio Free Europe (06.04.2026) wrote that it foresees about 15,000 electric cars by 2031. The two could not be reconciled. Source: me.rks-gov.net · medium reliability
  • Prishtina's 2021 e-taxi licensing call requires each licensed operator to install two DC chargers of at least 50 kW, one of them in public space and reported as free for all citizens; whether these chargers exist and are free today was not verified. Source: prishtinaonline.com · 02.11.2021 · medium reliability

What could not be verified

Known gaps. If you can fill one with a public source, write to hello@blokvolt.com.

  • ERO's 2025 tariff decision (V_2703_2025) publishes its tariff table as an image, so household prices come from media; the number of the 2026 decision was not found.
  • ERO and KESCO do not say whether the above-800 kWh prices apply only to the kWh above 800; the fixed monthly charge (€2.32) comes from a single source.
  • It is not stated whether ECHARGE prices include VAT, which sites get €0.39 vs €0.49, or which hours count as day and night.
  • The excise on imported electric cars comes from media only (€400–700 by age for used cars vs €400 per electric car); the customs tariff could not be read. Whether the Assembly passed the zero registration tax for electric cars was not checked.
  • The share of GB/T connectors among imported Chinese EVs is not documented.
  • No fast charger on the R6 or R7 motorways was confirmed. The latest source found on motorway tolls (Radio Free Europe, 16.05.2021) says they are toll-free; no 2025–2026 confirmation.
