For companies and suppliers

How to get a company or a charging network listed or corrected on BlokVolt, and where supplier quotations go.

Listing on BlokVolt

BlokVolt lists public charging networks and companies that sell or install home chargers, in Serbia and its neighbours. A listing is free. What a company or a network says on its own website goes into the same columns for everyone — what it sells, published prices, how to pay, where it works — with the date of the check. There are no ratings, no rankings and no paid placements.

To add your company or correct a detail, send the company name, website and city — or the correction with a link to where it is published — to hello@blokvolt.com. Serbian companies can also use the correction page on blokvolt.rs. Networks in the neighbouring countries can write in their own language.

Charging hardware suppliers

The installation business behind BlokVolt operates as Evolako and works in Serbia. Quotations, datasheets and partnership offers for wallboxes, sub-meters and accessories go to hello@evolako.com.

Being a supplier does not change a listing. Whether a company sells to Evolako has no effect on whether or how it appears on BlokVolt: every company is shown by the same public rules.
