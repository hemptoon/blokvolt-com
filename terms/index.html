Terms of use and privacy

What BlokVolt is

BlokVolt is a free reference on electric-vehicle charging: a map of charging places, what charging costs and how to pay, country by country. It is run by its founders.

The data comes from open sources and is shown as it is. A charger may have closed, changed or be out of order — check on site before you rely on it. Prices are for reference only: the price that applies is the one the operator's app or the charger shows.

BlokVolt is not connected with any charging operator or public body. Data from public bodies — for example the U.S. Department of Energy and NLR, MITECO in Spain, LTA in Singapore, Transport for NSW and the Government of Hong Kong — is used under their open licences; this does not mean that they endorse BlokVolt or check its pages.

Networks are not ranked and there are no paid placements.

Corrections

If a place is wrong, missing or closed, open it on the map: under "Is it working?" any driver can mark whether it works, without an account, and "Report an error" sends a correction. You can also write to hello@blokvolt.com.

Many places come from OpenStreetMap; where a card links to it, a correction there reaches every map that uses OpenStreetMap.

Privacy

Records of private persons are not shown on the map, and where a record of a charging place names a person, the name is masked.

Marks and error reports are stored without an account. To limit abuse, the server keeps a fingerprint made from the IP address with a random key that is valid for one day; the IP address itself is not stored.

To object to a record that concerns you or to ask for it to be removed (the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection, the GDPR), write to hello@blokvolt.com with the link to the place. The answer comes within 30 days.

There is no third-party analytics and no advertising on blokvolt.com. Visits are counted only by BlokVolt's own counter, on Cloudflare servers in the EU: which page was opened (without the parameters in the address), which website the visit came from (the site name only), campaign tags (utm), the page language, country, device type and browser. The counter sets no cookies and writes nothing to the browser. To count the same person once a day, an anonymous fingerprint is made from the IP address and browser data with a random key that is valid only for that day; the IP address is not stored. The data is used only in aggregate and is deleted after one year.
